Why this file exists: the pilots (PILOT-2026-11-17.md, PILOT-3-2026-11-17.md) flagged three

Notes and limits
- iNaturalist's taxon page shows the species as "Introduced" in Armenia via its own place
  checklist. That status can be created from observations themselves, so it is not treated as
  an independent report.
- EPPO's silverleaf nightshade list also omits Azerbaijan and Iran, where the species is
  published, so absence from EPPO alone is weak evidence for Armenia. The 2026 PhytoKeys
  revision is the stronger check. (The author list is not reproduced here; see the DOI.)
- No observer was contacted. Alert drafts for national authorities wait for Sheehan's approval
  and are not sent from this repository.
